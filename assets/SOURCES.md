# Asset sources

- ICML: official [press resources](https://icml.cc/public/PressPolicy), [source SVG](https://icml.cc/media/Press/ICML-logo.svg). The original head/squares symbol paths are extracted without redrawing. Dark-theme head fill is lightened for contrast; the original colored squares are preserved.
- NeurIPS: official [media kit](https://neurips.cc/public/MediaKit), [source SVG](https://neurips.cc/media/Press/NeurIPS_logo.svg). The two original purple symbol paths are extracted without redrawing; geometry is preserved; the purple fills are lightened in the dark variant for contrast. Badge text is exactly “NeurIPS 2026”.
- SIGMOD: custom generic database cylinder, as requested; not the official SIGMOD logo.
- Header wave field, section icons, and badge frames: custom SVG artwork.

Conference marks belong to their respective organizations and identify publication venues. Badge frames and added labels are custom, not official conference assets.
